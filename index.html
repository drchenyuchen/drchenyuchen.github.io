Bio

Chen-Yu Chen, Ph.D. is a Staff Research Engineer at the Robert E. Fischell Institute for Biomedical Devices and the Fischell Department of Bioengineering at the University of Maryland, College Park. His research centers on bioelectronics, redox-based electrochemical biosensing, and 3D-printed organ-on-a-chip and microfluidic systems for real-time biomanufacturing process analytical technology (PAT) and bidirectional electronic interrogation of living cells.

Bridging academic microsystem innovation with translational biotech R&D, Dr. Chen has authored 21 peer-reviewed journal publications and 3 US patent applications across electrode biofabrication, spectroelectrochemistry, and microphysiological platforms. Prior to his work at UMD and Cornell University, he led GMP/cGMP clinical-analytical method development, CMC scale-up, and FDA/EMA regulatory submissions for liposomal drug formulations at TLC BioSciences.
